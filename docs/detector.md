# E3: a defect detector trained only in thermoscene

Insulation voids in exterior walls of 76 EnergyPlus example buildings of distinct geometry: 45 for training (1800 views), 8 for validation, 23 unseen buildings for testing (920 views, 466 with a void whose true contrast is at least 0.5 K; true contrast of the test voids 0.13 / 1.27 / 3.78 K at the 10th / 50th / 90th percentile). Every detector is tested on realistic camera readings of the test buildings, with the decision threshold chosen on the validation buildings of its own training form. Mean ± standard deviation over 3 training seeds. Generated by `case_studies/detector_train.py`.

| Trained on | Threshold | Void-pixel AP | Instance F1 | Recall | Precision | False blobs per view without a void | F1 at the oracle threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| true temperature field | 0.95, 0.97, 0.97 | 0.50 ± 0.10 | 0.04 ± 0.07 | 0.05 ± 0.09 | 0.03 ± 0.06 | 0.07 ± 0.10 | 0.13 ± 0.10 |
| ideal camera, idealised scene | 0.95, 0.98, 0.97 | 0.50 ± 0.14 | 0.02 ± 0.03 | 0.02 ± 0.03 | 0.02 ± 0.03 | 0.03 ± 0.03 | 0.16 ± 0.14 |
| realistic readings (thermoscene) | 0.95, 0.95, 0.95 | 0.86 ± 0.02 | 0.51 ± 0.01 | 0.79 ± 0.03 | 0.38 ± 0.02 | 0.75 ± 0.16 | 0.51 ± 0.01 |
| no training: local anomaly (15 x 15 median, noise units) | 4 | 0.085 | 0.002 | 0.013 | 0.001 | 8.1 | – |
| no training: global hot/cold spot (kelvin from image median) | 1.5 | 0.201 | 0.06 | 0.536 | 0.032 | 9.375 | – |

The oracle threshold is the one that is best on the realistic test images themselves, which no developer without field data can choose; it shows how much of the gap a better calibration alone could close.
