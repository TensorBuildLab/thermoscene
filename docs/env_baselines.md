# E4: agents in the inspection environment (fault search: the wall void of cases B and C)

30 episodes of at most 60 steps per agent and building; random start pose, time step and camera unit (160 x 120 class, one sample per pixel), the same episodes for every agent. Every detector steers the same search: declare a centred candidate, otherwise turn and tilt to centre the largest one (declaring after at most 4 corrections), otherwise turn 30 degrees and move forward after a full turn; directions already declared without a fault are skipped. The learned agent's detector was trained only on thermoscene scenes of other buildings (`docs/detector.md`, threshold 0.95); the hot/cold-spot agent uses the better label-free detector of that experiment (threshold 1.5 K). The agents with detectors trained on true temperature fields or on idealised images use the same network trained in that experiment on those forms of the same views (first seed, their own validation thresholds), fed the same camera readings. Generated by `case_studies/env_baselines.py`.

## Notes

About 0.212 s per environment step on one laptop core (wall-clock, other jobs running).

A first version of the search turned in fixed 30-degree steps toward the largest blob and looked away for three steps after a false declaration. It swung between two headings when a target lay between them and missed the void when it came into view right after a false declaration, so that even the agent given the labels found the void of case B in only 12 of 30 episodes. It was replaced by the search described above, and all agents were re-run on the same episodes; the first results are superseded.
